    text: |-
      Nine grimlocks came up the gallery, and behind them two dark dwarves riding
      giant spiders, and we stood in the dark counting them and doing the only sum
      that matters.

      We put it to a vote. That is not a figure of speech. We stopped, in a tunnel,
      with torchlight coming up the gallery, and we took a vote.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Four to one.

      I was the one. I would like to be able to tell you I had a reason — a plan, a
      bottleneck, something about the ground. I did not. I wanted to stay because we
      had dug through a wall for two days and been strengthened in a holy pool and
      knelt at a broken shrine, and leaving felt like agreeing that none of it had
      counted.

      That is not a reason. That is a feeling with its boots on.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      So we ran.

      All the way back. Down the climb we had come up, across the water we had
      crossed, through rooms we had bled in a day earlier and now went through at a
      flat sprint, with Grahl behind us the whole way and gaining on the straights.

      At the bottom there were tunnels, and I picked ours. I picked it precisely
      because it was the one nobody wanted — wet, close, and smelling so comprehensively
      of something's lavatory that pursuit became a matter of enthusiasm rather than
      duty. It worked. It is, to this day, the single best decision I have made
      underground, and I made it by asking myself which way I would least like to go.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      My note for that day, written at the time, in full, reads: *fuck this.*

      I have decided to leave it in.
