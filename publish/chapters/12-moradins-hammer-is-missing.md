  - type: telling
    text: |-
      You can feel the moment the work changes. The tunnels we had been crawling
      through were somebody's cellar and somebody's mine and somebody's hole, and
      then a corner turns and the walls are squared and the lintels are dressed and
      the whole thing is old dwarven work, done properly, by people who expected it
      to outlast them.

      It has outlasted them. That is rather the point.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      There was a shrine to Moradin a little way in, and it had been broken on
      purpose. Not looted — broken. Somebody had spent time on it. The hammer that
      should have been set above the stone was gone, and the socket it came out of
      had been worked at with a chisel.

      Magnus knelt. Of course Magnus knelt.

      I knelt beside him, and I want to be accurate about this because it surprised
      me more than it surprised him: I did not decide to. I am not a devout dwarf. I
      am barely a respectable one. My knees went down on the stone of their own
      accord and stayed there while an old man said words over a smashed altar a
      thousand miles from anyone who would care, and then we both got up and neither
      of us mentioned it.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Then the city tried to kill us.

      Griks first, then grimlocks, then quaggoths, which is a sequence I would not
      have arranged in that order. And then one of the dark dwarves got bigger.

      I do not mean he stood up. I mean the duergar grew, there, in front of us,
      doubling, and the room stopped being a room we could fight in and became a
      room we were inside.

      Magnus went down. Shae went down. Between the two of them they failed three
      saving throws while the rest of us were busy, and I have never been able to
      decide whether telling you that is honest or obscene.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Garnel killed it.

      The gnome from the alcove. A ray of frost, at the exact moment, as well as it
      is possible to do that, and the giant thing came apart and the fight was over
      and we were all still alive by a margin so thin it does not bear examining.

      Somehow, we survived. I wrote that down at the time and I have not been able
      to improve on it since.
