blocks:
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      You smell a troglodyte settlement a long time before you reach it, and after
      two days in the lavatory tunnel we had lost the right to be superior about it.

      There were pens. There were things in the pens.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Two lizardfolk, Kor and Mimi. An elf who had been in there long enough that
      we got nothing out of him for an hour. And a mushroom man — I have no better
      word, and nor did he — with four small spores that moved when he moved and
      stopped when he stopped, like ducklings.

      We let them out because that is what you do, and I would like to report that
      we did it out of decency and not because five is a small number and we had
      just run away from a city.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      What happened next I did not expect and have not forgotten.

      They did not leave. They did not scatter into the dark the moment the pens
      were open, which is what I would have done and what I was braced for. They
      looked at the five of us, and at the way we had come in, and they went to
      work.

      Kor and Mimi started rebuilding the barricade. The elf laid traps on the
      approach — proper ones, patient ones, the work of somebody who had been
      watching his captors move for a long time and had been planning this in his
      head for weeks. The mushroom man put his spores where they could see both
      doors.

      Within the hour we had a defensible position and four allies, and not one of
      us had asked for either.
