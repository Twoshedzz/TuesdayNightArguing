    text: |-
      There was a cave off the road that looked dry, and dry had become the only
      quality any of us cared about. There were two owlbears in it. One awake, one
      asleep.

      What should have happened next is that we walked away.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      What happened instead is the longest conversation of the entire expedition,
      and not one second of it was about tactics.

      Somebody — and the notes are deliberately vague about who, which tells you
      everything — proposed that we put the bag of holding over the sleeping
      owlbear's head.

      This was not dismissed. This was debated. At length, in whispers, forty feet
      from two owlbears, by five adults who had survived a near-total wipe
      underground the previous week. Points were made about the mouth of the bag.
      Points were made about the size of an owlbear's head relative to the mouth of
      the bag. Somebody raised the question of what would happen to the rest of the
      owlbear.
  - type: interruption
    channel: "general"
    lines: |-
      *game paused*
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Magnus ended the argument by rolling the worst stealth check I have ever
      witnessed, in full plate, on gravel.

      Both owlbears were awake after that and the question became academic. We
      fought them in the mouth of the cave where they could only come at us one at
      a time, which is the plan we should have arrived at an hour earlier and would
      have, if the bag had not been in the conversation.

      I have kept the bag. It has never been over anything's head. I consider this
      an open question.
