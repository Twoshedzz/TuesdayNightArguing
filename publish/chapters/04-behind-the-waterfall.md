      The standing stones sit on both banks, one set facing the other across the
      water, and whatever they were put there to mark has been gone for a thousand
      years. Beyond them the cliffs. Merlin sent his hawk up and we had a good look
      at the ground, and we knew — we actually knew, we had been told and we had
      paid five gold to be told — that there was a vantage point above the path.

      I voted for the caves. I was the only one.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Goblin archers opened on us from the high ground and put Shae down in the
      first seconds, which is the fastest I have ever seen anyone go from upright to
      not. Then Muley panicked.

      I want to be fair to the mule. He was being shot at. But he chose, out of
      everything available to him on that path, to put his shoulder into Magnus and
      send a dwarf in full plate over the edge of a cliff.

      He went over. He got a hand to the rock. And I sang, because singing is what
      I have, and whatever it was that I did went into his arms and held him there
      until Torgan could reach him. I have never seen a man that angry while
      thanking somebody.

  - type: telling
    text: |-
      The path to the ruin runs behind the waterfall, and it is wet, and it is
      narrow, and it slopes.

      I am a mountain dwarf. Stone is the one thing I am supposed to be able to do.
      I went down almost at once, and I could not get back up, and after a certain
      number of attempts there is no dignity left to protect, so I crawled. On my
      hands and knees. Through the water. For the entire length of the path, in
      full view of four people who had just watched me save a man's life and were
      now watching this.

      Nobody laughed. That was worse. Torgan put a hand out at the far end and I
      took it, and we all agreed without saying anything that it had not happened,
      and then of course it had, and it is in the book now, so it always will have.
