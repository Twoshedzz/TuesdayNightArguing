      The way on was barricaded from the far side, and we took it down with two
      spells at once — Garnel's chromatic orb into the centre of it and my
      thunderwave on top, timed by counting, which worked better than it had any
      right to.

      The hall behind it was full.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      The shaman put out a yellow gas that took half of us out of the fight
      immediately. Not killed — retching, on hands and knees, unable to do anything
      but be sick while the room went on without us. I was one of them for part of
      it. I have been afraid in a great many fights and that was the only one where
      I was humiliated at the same time.

      The chieftain came through the middle of it and killed the mushroom man.

      And most of the spores.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      I want to set down what that actually means, because a sentence like *we
      rescued some prisoners and they helped us* is a lie by omission.

      We opened their pens. They chose to stay. They fixed our barricade and laid
      our traps and stood in our line, and when the chieftain came through, the
      thing that died first was the one of them with the least reason to be there
      and the most to lose. His spores went with him, except one.

      We won the hall. That is the word for it in the record. We won the hall.
