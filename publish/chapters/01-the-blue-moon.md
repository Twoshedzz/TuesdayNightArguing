    text: |-
      We met on a boat, which is not the beginning anybody would choose. Four
      people with four reasons for crossing the sea, and none of us asked each
      other what they were, because on a boat you are already trapped together and
      questions make it worse.

      Fallcrest took us in the way a town takes in anyone with coin. There is a
      wealthy half up on the rise and a poor half spread along the water, and we
      drank in the poor half, at the Blue Moon Alehouse, for the better part of
      three weeks. Torgan ate. Merlin read. Shae went quiet in corners and came
      back knowing things. I played, badly at first and then better, and the
      landlord stopped charging me, which in my profession counts as critical
      acclaim.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Magnus found us rather than the other way round. An old dwarf off the guard
      tower who drank alone and did not mind company, which is the rarest
      combination there is. He had the look of a man who had been devout for so
      long that it had stopped being a decision. It was Magnus who heard there was
      work, and Magnus who brought the work to our table in the shape of a nervous
      young halfling called Tobs.

      Tobs Quickfoot was apprenticed to the wizard Nimozaran, who nobody ever sees,
      and who keeps a well-preserved greenstone tower that everybody can see.
      Apprentice is a generous word. Tobs ran errands. One of the errands was us.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      He got about four sentences into it before five men came through the door.

      I want to be accurate about this, because it is the first thing we ever did
      together and it set the pattern for everything after. We did not fight well.
      Torgan swung and connected and that was the high point. I threw a handaxe at
      a man eight feet away and missed him, picked it up, threw it again, and
      missed him again. What I did do — three times, and it is the only reason this
      is a story and not an inquest — was sing. Bardic inspiration is a ridiculous
      thing to put your faith in and it worked, and the men who had come to kill
      the halfling went down or ran.

      Tobs was unconscious by then. I got him breathing. He came round, looked at
      the bodies, and said it was not the first time this had happened over this
      job, which is the sort of thing an employer should mention before the
      interview rather than after.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      One of the five got out of the door and away down the hill. Nobody chased
      him. We stood in a pub we had been drinking in for three weeks with the floor
      wet and a halfling counting his own teeth, and we had not yet agreed to work
      together, or decided we liked each other, or learned one another's full names.

      I have thought about that evening a great deal since, mostly in the dark, and
      what stays with me is not the fight. It is that the bar we drank in was the
      bar we bled in, and that we went back the next night, and the night after
      that, and the landlord had cleaned the boards and said nothing about it.
