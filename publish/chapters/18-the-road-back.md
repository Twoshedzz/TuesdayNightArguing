  - type: telling
    text: |-
      We were four days out and thinking about beds.

      That is when it happened, obviously. It is always when you are thinking about
      beds.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      An ogre first, with bugbears behind it, which is a shape of fight we had
      learned by then — you keep the big one occupied and you deal with the small
      ones before they get round the sides.

      The rogue was not part of that plan. He came in off the flank and put Shae
      down, and then Torgan, in about the time it takes to describe it. Two of our
      five, on the ground, in seconds, by one man working quickly and well.

      And then he ran.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      No speech. No demand. He had done what he came to do, and what he came to do
      was that, and then he was gone into the scrub and we were standing in the road
      with two people bleeding and nothing to chase.

      We got them both back. Magnus earned his share of the eighty gold that
      afternoon several times over.

      I have never found out who he was. I have an idea, and the idea involves a
      woman tied to a post and a man who got out of the door of the Blue Moon
      Alehouse on the first night and went down the hill, and I have no evidence for
      any of it, and I have never said it out loud to the others.
