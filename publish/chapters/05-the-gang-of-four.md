  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Merlin's hawk found the riders before we did. Three of them, north and east,
      moving at a speed that has nothing to do with horses. Wolves, ridden. They
      were there and then they were not, and we stood in the open for a while
      afterwards saying nothing useful.

      Closer in, the crows. A great raft of them lifting off something and settling
      again and lifting off, and one bigger bird in among them taking exception.
      Underneath all of that was a lizardman, or two halves of one. Not eaten.
      Pulled apart, and left.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      There was smoke coming off the second tower, which meant people, which meant
      the ruin we had walked sixty-two miles to rob was already occupied.

      They came out at us in a rush. A fat man with a club, swinging it like a
      farmer clearing scrub. A longbowman — Bram, we learned his name afterwards,
      which I would rather we had not. And a woman who shouted, right into the
      middle of it, in the voice of someone announcing a travelling show:

      *We're the gang of four!*
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      There were not four of them. I counted at the time and I have counted since.

      Magnus killed Bram with the maul and it was not clean. There is a particular
      silence that follows that sort of injury, where everybody present understands
      that the thing on the ground was recently making plans. Then we went back to
      work, because the woman was still up, and the fat man was still swinging, and
      a fight does not pause for anyone's feelings.

      When it stopped, we had one of them alive.
