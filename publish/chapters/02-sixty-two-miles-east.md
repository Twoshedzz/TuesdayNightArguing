      The job was stones.

      Sixty-two miles east, in a ruin called Ethium, there were old blue stones
      worth fifty gold each to somebody who was not us. Bring back as many as we
      could carry. That was the whole of it, and if it sounds thin written down,
      it sounded thin said aloud as well, which is why we went to the keep.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      I do not enjoy being useful in rooms. Give me a tunnel and a problem and I am
      a happy dwarf. Put me in front of a mayor with an audience and a polished
      floor and I become aware of my own hands. But a bard is a bard, and the mayor
      came into the room braced for a delegation of mercenaries and left it having
      agreed to eighty gold, a letter of passage, and a bounty.

      The bounty is the part I would ask you to remember.

      Three gold a goblin. Five an orc. Eight a bugbear. Paid on the ear.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Nobody objected. I did not object. It was framed as pest control by a
      reasonable man behind a desk, and we were four strangers and a priest with
      eighty gold and a long road, and the arithmetic was obvious, and we all did
      the arithmetic.

      I will come back to this.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      On the way out a messenger sold us the road for five gold — where the water
      was, where the ground turned, which of the three ways east was the one that
      did not get you killed. Five gold for a conversation. Torgan called it
      robbery. It was the best money we spent on the entire expedition, and I
      include the healing potions.

      We left the next morning with a mule called Muley, who hated me on sight and
      never revised that opinion, and who will shortly nearly kill a priest.
