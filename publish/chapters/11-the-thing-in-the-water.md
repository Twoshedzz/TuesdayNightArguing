      not my idea. There is a finite quantity of air inside a bag of holding and an infinite
      quantity of ways to die in the dark, and we elected to combine them. Merlin went down

      himself, fell again, and landed with a crunch every one of us heard.

      Below us the water did not move the way water moves. It carried a skin of filth across
      it, and under the filth something took up more room than it ought to have.

      stop talking, and so I gave Torgan my voice for the third time that night, and he came

      eight days earlier, who had spent the whole fight backed against the rock shouting a

      light through the thing's skull.
