      The tunnel I had chosen went on a great deal longer than any of us had
      budgeted for, and after a while it began to glow.

      Fungus. Pale, cold, in sheets along the lower walls, enough of it to see by
      once your eyes gave up on torchlight. Everything in that light is the wrong
      colour. Torgan looked like a drowned man for two days and took it personally.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Carrion crawlers found us in the narrow section, which is their preference
      and not ours. They paralyse. There is a particular horror in watching
      somebody you know stop being able to move while a thing with that many legs
      works out what to do about it, and we got Shae back, and we were lucky.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      Further on there was a thing propped up on sticks.

      An insectoid, dead, enormous, arranged — and I use that word carefully —
      arranged upright at a junction with lizard skulls set around its base in a
      ring. It had not been there long. The skulls were clean and the sticks were
      green.

      Nobody says anything clever when they find a totem. You stand in front of it
      and you understand that you are the stranger here and that whoever built it
      meant it to be understood, and then you go on past it because the alternative
      is going back, and we had voted on that already.
