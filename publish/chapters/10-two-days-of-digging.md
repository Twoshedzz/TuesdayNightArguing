      There was no door. There was a wall with the wrong sort of rubble behind it,
      which to a dwarf is a door that has not been argued with yet, and so we dug.

      Two days. I want that to land properly, because adventuring stories skip it
      and then wonder why nobody believes them. Two days of moving stone out of a
      hole by hand, in shifts, in the dark, underground, with five people and no
      tools worth the name. Garnel sang at one point. It did not help and we did not
      ask him to stop.
  - type: telling
    text: |-
      The bugbears came on the second night, which was when we were least able to
      do anything about it. An arrow came out of the black and went into Magnus and
      the first anybody knew about any of it was the noise he made.

      There was a short, ugly scramble in the dark with nobody able to see what they
      were swinging at. Then the goblins with them decided it was not worth it and
      left, which is a thing goblins do, and which we would be very grateful for
      later and very annoyed about at the time.

      We patched Magnus. We went back to digging. I have a clear memory of Torgan
      pulling a rock out of the wall with an arrow still being removed from his
      priest four feet behind him, and of nobody finding that strange.
